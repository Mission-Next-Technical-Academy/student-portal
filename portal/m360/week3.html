Week Brief

Build relationships before you need them.

Networking should help you learn faster, understand the work more clearly, and create informed next steps—not collect contacts or disguise a job request.

Learn It

Treat networking as a learning system.

The goal is not volume. Build a small relevant map, make the ask easy to answer, and create continuity after the first action.

01
Learning system

Useful connections help you test assumptions.

Use practitioners, peers, instructors, employers, recruiters, alumni, associations, events, and communities to understand roles, skills, tools, expectations, and possible next moves.

02
Map first

Know why each target matters before contacting them.

For every target, identify what you want to learn, the best connection method, your current status, and the next action.

03
Outreach

Context → Curiosity → Clear close.

Explain why the person or community is relevant, ask one focused question, and make the next step easy to accept, answer, or decline.

04
Informational interview

Ask to learn—not to disguise a job request.

A short conversation can clarify what the work looks like, which skills matter, how people enter the field, and what strong entry-level preparation looks like.

05
Continuity + privacy

Follow up intentionally and protect other people’s information.

Track the action, record the insight, decide what comes next, and remove unnecessary personal or sensitive information from evidence you submit.

Live Instruction

Use live time to refine the map, messages, and next move.

Session 1 teaches and applies the networking model. Session 2 is coaching and portfolio building inside Practice It.

Session 1 · Instruction · 60 min

Map useful relationships and make the ask credible.

  • Connect Week 1 direction and Week 2 professional presence to networking targets.
  • Refine at least seven relevant targets.
  • Workshop three types of outreach.
  • Choose one realistic action to complete.
Session 2 · Coaching · 60 min

Practice, act, document, and prepare the Week 3 proof.

  • Work directly in the Practice It workspace.
  • Review message clarity and evidence integrity.
  • Sanitize real-action evidence before submission.
  • Create the follow-up plan and reflection.
