Learn It

Build one coherent professional signal.

Keep the learning short and practical. The live session is where you test language, ask questions, and apply it to your actual profile.

01
Search result

Your profile should make sense without an introduction.

A useful profile quickly communicates who you are professionally, where you are headed, what evidence supports that direction, and what someone should remember.

10-second profile test

Can a stranger identify your target direction, two useful capabilities, one credible proof point, and a reasonable way to connect with you?

02
Headline

Create direction, not title noise.

Broad

“Professional | Leader | Veteran | Student | Problem Solver | Open to Work”

Focused

“IT Support Professional | Troubleshooting, User Support & Service Documentation”

The stronger version is easier to understand and connect to a target role. It does not rely on sounding more impressive.

03
About

Connect direction, strengths, and evidence.

Use your Week 1 professional brand as the starting point. Open with direction, add two or three relevant capabilities with evidence, then close with your current learning, work, or next-step focus.

Keep it credible

Avoid generic claims, long autobiography, unsupported superlatives, and language that does not sound like you.

04
Evidence

Show capability, not only responsibility.

Responsibility

“Responsible for handling support tickets and assisting users.”

Evidence

“Resolved user support requests by gathering symptoms, documenting troubleshooting steps, and escalating issues when needed to maintain clear service continuity.”

05
Keywords + privacy

Every skill is a claim. Every public field is a choice.

Use accurate keywords supported by experience, coursework, projects, or technical practice. Review visibility, contact details, location, links, and visuals intentionally. A profile photo is optional and student-controlled.

Live Instruction

Use live time to test the signal and improve the actual profile.

Session 1 teaches and applies the concepts. Session 2 is instructor coaching and portfolio building in the Practice It workspace.

Session 1 · Instruction · 60 min

Diagnose clarity, then revise the profile.

  • Connect Week 1 direction to profile positioning.
  • Workshop headlines and About-section structure.
  • Translate one experience entry into visible capability.
  • Identify 5–8 accurate target-direction keywords.
Session 2 · Coaching · 60 min

Practice, build, verify, and prepare the Week 2 proof.

  • Work directly in the Practice It workspace.
  • Compare draft language to the actual LinkedIn profile.
  • Check evidence integrity, public presentation, and privacy choices.
  • Prepare the required submission for Prove It.
