Live sessions + LMS

Both parts of M360 have a job.

Live sessions

Practice, discussion, instructor coaching, portfolio building, and the Career Spotlight presentation happen live.

LMS

Complete lessons, save work, submit assignments, receive feedback, track progress, and preserve accepted portfolio evidence here.

M360 attendance is documented outside the LMS. Portal activity, browser time, or lesson completion is not treated as M360 instructional time.
Grading + completion

Start Here is setup, not a graded instructional week.

  • Weeks 1–6 are graded; Start Here is not.
  • All six required weekly artifacts must be completed.
  • The final M360 average must be at least 70%.
  • Work may be returned for revision before it Meets Standard.
  • Career Spotlight and the applicable live-session attendance requirement must be completed.
Portfolio + final proof

Your accepted work builds one M360 Portfolio.

M360 Portfolio

A simple progress and evidence record that preserves accepted artifacts as your work develops. It is not a public employer profile for the MVP.

Career Spotlight

In Week 6, you deliver a five-minute Career Spotlight using no more than three slides. Live presentation is the default; authorized staff record the presentation completion status.
