What does “done” look like?

You do not have to nail everything on the first try.

M360 is built around practice, feedback, and stronger second drafts — not one-and-done assignments.

  • Start Here gets you set up. There is no grade attached.
  • Weeks 1–6 each end with one required artifact. Those six assignments make up your M360 grade.
  • 70% is the minimum final average. If work needs another pass, your instructor can return it for revision before it becomes Portfolio Ready.
  • Week 6 brings everything together. Finish your Career Spotlight and the required live-session participation to complete M360.
