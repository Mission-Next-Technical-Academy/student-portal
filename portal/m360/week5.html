Week Brief

Strong interviews are built on specific examples and practice.

Your job is not to memorize perfect paragraphs. Build a small bank of credible examples, organize them with STAR, and practice adapting them to different questions.

Choose proofStart with resume bullets, labs, projects, prior work, and professional growth.
StructureUse STAR bullets to keep context, action, and outcome clear.
PracticePractice at least one answer live, with a peer/instructor, or through an approved reflection process.
ImproveCapture feedback or self-observation and decide what to practice next.

Learn It

Interview answers need observable proof.

STAR helps organize evidence. It should make your answer easier to follow, not make you sound scripted.

01
Proof

Give the interviewer an example they can understand.

Show what happened, what you did, how you thought, and what the example demonstrates.

02
STAR

Situation · Task/context · Action · Result/learning.

Use concise bullets to organize the story, then speak in plain language.

03
Competencies

Build reusable stories around behaviors employers ask about.

Problem-solving, communication, teamwork, initiative, learning, user support, technical troubleshooting, analyst thinking, and professional judgment are common categories.

04
Practice loop

Answer → observe → revise → practice again.

Useful feedback focuses on clarity, relevance, evidence, and what the listener can actually understand.

Live Instruction

Use live time to build, test, and refine your stories.

Session 1 teaches story selection and STAR structure. Session 2 is coaching and practice inside the portfolio-build workspace.

Session 1 · Instruction · 60 min

Turn written evidence into interview-ready examples.

  • Identify useful story categories.
  • Convert evidence bullets into STAR structure.
  • Separate supported outcomes from assumptions.
  • Practice concise, natural delivery.
Session 2 · Coaching · 60 min

Practice one answer and strengthen the story bank.

  • Work directly in the Week 5 workspace.
  • Test one or more stories against likely questions.
  • Capture feedback or self-observation.
  • Finish interviewer questions and improvement plan.
M360 attendance and clock-hour documentation remain outside the LMS. No recording is required for Week 5 unless a separately approved activity explicitly says otherwise.
