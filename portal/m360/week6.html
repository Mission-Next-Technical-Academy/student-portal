Week Brief

Your final proof point is a professional story, not a life-story deck.

Career Spotlight combines direction, evidence, communication, and next action. Production polish is secondary to clarity and real proof.

3 slides totalNo separate title slide. A small identification area may appear on Slide 1.
5 minutesLive is the default unless an approved exception applies.
Real evidenceDraw from accepted M360 artifacts rather than unsupported claims.
30-day planFinish with three concrete actions and clear accountability.
Learn It

Three slides are enough when each slide has one job.

01
Slide 1

Who I am / where I am going.

State your professional direction and the contribution you are building toward.

02
Slide 2

What I can do / proof.

Choose one supportable example that demonstrates capability and how you work.

03
Slide 3

What is next / 30-day plan.

Show the next target and the actions you will take immediately after M360.

04
Communication

Use the slides as support, not a script.

Professional Communication is a separate 20-point rubric dimension.

Live Instruction

Build, rehearse, and deliver the Career Spotlight.

Session 1 · Instruction · 60 min

Assemble the three-slide story.

  • Select the strongest accepted evidence.
  • Keep one clear purpose per slide.
  • Connect direction → proof → next step.
  • Practice a five-minute delivery.
Session 2 · Coaching · 60 min

Refine and prepare to prove it.

  • Work directly in the Week 6 workspace.
  • Test clarity and evidence with instructor coaching.
  • Finalize the action plan and reflection.
  • Present live or document an approved exception path.
M360 attendance remains documented outside the LMS. The LMS stores only the approved staff-confirmed attendance requirement bridge for final course completion.
