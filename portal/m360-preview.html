Learn It

Learn a few useful ideas before the live session.

This section is intentionally short. Learn the concepts, inspect strong examples, and bring questions to live instruction. The live session connects the pieces before you build.

01
Direction

Direction is a working hypothesis.

You do not need a perfect job title. You need a next move specific enough to guide what you practice, what evidence you collect, and what you investigate next.

Broad

“I want to work in technology.”

Focused

“I am exploring entry-level support roles where I can diagnose user issues, document fixes, and build toward systems administration.”

Why the focused version works

It names a starting lane, useful work, and a logical next direction without pretending the decision is permanent.

02
Evidence

Strengths require receipts.

A capability becomes credible when you can point to where and how you used it. Replace broad adjectives with a capability, context, and proof.

Weak

“I am a strong problem solver.”

Stronger

“I troubleshoot unclear issues by asking focused questions, testing likely causes, and documenting the resolution.”

What makes evidence useful

Use examples from work, school, labs, projects, volunteering, caregiving, community leadership, or current coursework. The proof should be yours and supportable.

Week 1 resources

Keep these open during instruction and coaching.

A Direction Decision CardCompare options without forcing certainty.

Compare a direction using five criteria:

  • Interest
  • Demonstrated strengths
  • Entry requirements
  • Access to practice
  • Next-step clarity

A low score is not failure. It tells you what to investigate.

B Translation + Brand CheckKeep claims accurate and understandable.

Translation: context → action → result/learning → capability.

10-second check: Can someone identify your direction, useful work, and evidence after one read?

If the language could describe almost anyone, add specificity. If it sounds inflated, narrow the claim.

Live Instruction

Use the live sessions to connect, question, and refine.

The pre-work gives you the pieces. Live instruction ties them together; the coaching session moves directly into the portfolio build.

Session 1 · Instruction · 60 min

Connect direction, evidence, and professional language.

  • Test a working direction without forcing certainty.
  • Diagnose vague claims and unsupported strengths.
  • Review examples across Help Desk, SOC, AI/ML, and career-pivot contexts.
  • Translate experience accurately for a professional audience.
Session 2 · Coaching · 60 min

Build the Week 1 portfolio artifact with an instructor.

  • Work directly in the Build It workspace.
  • Challenge vague language with “What is the example behind that?”
  • Refine the brand statement until it is specific, believable, and reusable.
  • Prepare the artifact for instructor review and acceptance.
