Week Brief

Start with direction, not a perfect job title.

You do not need every answer today. You do need a useful starting point you can test, refine, and support with evidence.

Direction: name the role family, problem space, environment, tools, or kind of work you are moving toward.
Evidence: identify at least three strengths or capabilities and the examples that make them believable.
Translation: rewrite one experience in plain professional language without unexplained jargon.
Brand: draft a clear statement that connects your direction, strengths, and evidence.
Learn It

Your professional brand is a pattern, not a slogan.

A useful brand helps someone understand the kind of work you are moving toward, the capabilities you bring, and the evidence behind those claims.

Use one rule throughout this week

Evidence beats adjectives. Replace vague claims with examples, outcomes, tools, decisions, responsibilities, or work products that show the capability.

Exploration is acceptable. If you are still deciding, name what you are exploring. The goal is a direction strong enough to guide LinkedIn, resume, networking, and interview work later in M360.
