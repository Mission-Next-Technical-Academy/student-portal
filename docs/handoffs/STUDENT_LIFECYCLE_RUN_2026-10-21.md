## Persistence acceptance criteria for every module

Persistence is the central UAT requirement, not a spot check of one field. This explicitly includes **all quiz questions and answers** (each selected answer, free-text response, confidence/feedback field, and quiz progress/result); **all lab inputs** (query/script/editor contents, names, saved/scheduled queries and schedules, rule/configuration fields, pins and unpins, evidence selections, verdicts and rationales, notes, ticket fields, dropdowns, checkboxes, toggles, automation payloads/bodies, guide steps, workflow choices, and every other text/number/date input); and **all Prove It inputs and state** (answers, evidence pins, written responses, checklist choices, draft/submission state, attempt number, score, and instructor review status). A lab inventory must specifically count and name pins, queries, schedules, quiz answers, and every text/select/check control present. Do not assume fields are covered because a whole panel looks saved.

For every editable control, enter a distinguishable value/selection and save through the UI; reopen the same panel/sheet and verify that control before proceeding. **At minimum, perform one full student-tab `localStorage` clear (preserve the signed-in session), refresh, reopen the module, and recheck every inventoried control and saved artifact for that module.** If any value is edited, newly saved, submitted, returned, or reviewed after that sweep, do another clear-and-refresh cycle for the changed surface and recheck its full inventory. Clear only after noting the existing local/session storage state, preserve the existing authentication session, and confirm the same student is still signed in. A toast, click response, restored shell, or an unrelated saved field is not proof. Confirm saved queries and enabled schedules remain attached to the student/case and that execution history, pins, messages, and attempt counts still reflect the correct records after reload. Record the per-surface input inventory and a field-by-field result in this log; explicitly mark inaccessible, blanked, or unverifiable fields as gaps. A module is not persistence-complete until its full input inventory is checked. The localStorage wipe tests recovery from browser-local state; the saved UI values after reload are the persistence evidence. No database commands or data fixes are permitted during this UAT.
